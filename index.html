📈 What to Expect: Weekly Growth Timeline

Week 1-2: Germination & Seedling

  • Seeds sprout in 3-10 days
  • First cotyledon (seed) leaves appear
  • Keep soil moist, not soggy
  • Provide 14-16 hours of light

Week 3: True Leaves Develop

  • First true leaves form (look like mini versions of adult leaves)
  • Seedlings grow stronger stems
  • Begin weak fertilizer solution (1/4 strength)
  • Hardening off prep: reduce watering slightly

Week 4: Transplant Ready

  • 2-3 true leaves visible
  • Strong, stocky stems
  • Roots established but not root-bound
  • TRANSPLANT WEEK! (if temps allow)

Week 5-6: Establishment

  • Plants adapt to outdoor conditions
  • Rapid vine growth begins
  • Increase watering as plants grow
  • Apply balanced fertilizer

Week 7-8: Flowering

  • First flower buds appear
  • Male flowers open first
  • Pollinators visit garden
  • Maintain consistent watering

Week 9-10: First Harvest!

  • Cucumbers: 50-70 days from seed
  • Zucchini: 50-60 days from seed
  • Harvest when small for best flavor
  • Pick regularly to encourage more production

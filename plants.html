Garden Box Layouts

Box 1: The Vertical Box

6ft × 7ft = 42 sq ft

  • Tomatoes: 4-6 plants with cages
  • Cucumbers: 4-6 plants on trellis
  • Carrots: Sow 100+, thin to 50-100
  • Arugula: Scatter in gaps

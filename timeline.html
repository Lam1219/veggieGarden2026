Save Tomato Seeds

Fermentation Method

1

Choose Fruit

Pick fully ripe, healthy tomato from vigorous plant. Only heirlooms.

2

Extract

Cut tomato, squeeze seeds + pulp into jar. Label immediately.

3

Ferment

Add splash of water. Cover loosely. Keep warm 3-5 days. Stir daily.

4

Rinse

Viable seeds sink. Pour into strainer. Rinse thoroughly.

5

Dry

Spread on paper towel. Dry 1-2 weeks. Seeds should snap, not bend.

6

Store

Paper envelope, label with variety + date. Store cool, dark, dry.
